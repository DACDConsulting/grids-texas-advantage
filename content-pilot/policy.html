Texas Policy Does Not Start in January

Success in Texas requires understanding where data center strategy meets the realities of the Texas market.

Most organizations watch legislation when bills are filed. The companies that gain a competitive advantage start much earlier.

Long before the legislative session convenes, lawmakers, regulators, agency leaders, stakeholders and industry groups are shaping the policy conversations that ultimately determine how data center development will be regulated across Texas. Water usage, power demand, tax incentives, permitting, infrastructure investment and community impact have all become increasingly important topics in Austin.

For developers, operators, investors, property owners and their counsel, understanding those conversations early can be the difference between reacting to policy and helping shape the environment in which projects operate.

The Texas Advantage comes from understanding not only what is happening in Austin, but why it is happening, who is driving the discussion and how decisions being made today may affect projects tomorrow.

Through its GRIDS capability, Gray Reed and GRPR help clients monitor legislative developments, evaluate regulatory risk, understand emerging policy trends and engage with the decision makers shaping the future of Texas infrastructure.
